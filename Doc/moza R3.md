## 2. Physical Setup

### 2.1 Mounting the Wheelbase

The R3 wheelbase is incredibly compact and can be mounted in two ways:

**Option A: Desk Mount (Recommended for Beginners)**

1. Attach the included table clamp to the underside of the R3 base using the 4 mounting holes
2. Secure the clamp to your desk
3. Tighten firmly—the clamp is designed to handle the R3's 3.9 Nm torque and supports desks up to 50mm thick

**Option B: Sim Rig Mount**

1. Use the 4-hole bottom mounting pattern (compatible with most sim racing cockpits and wheel stands)
2. Secure with appropriate bolts

> *"The clamp that comes with the R3 is so sturdy and accessible that removing the wheelbase from your desk is a piece of cake."* — Sim Racing Review

### 2.2 Positioning the Wheel

> *"Sit so your arms are slightly bent at 90° when holding the wheel."* — Apex Motor Psychology

**Correct Position:**
- Your arms should be slightly bent (about 90°) when holding the wheel at 9 and 3 o'clock
- Your shoulders should be relaxed, not hunched
- The wheel should be at a slight upward angle (like a real car)
- Your eyes should be level with the center of your screen

### 2.3 Positioning the Pedals

The SR-P Lite pedals feature fully adjustable pedal spacing:

1. Position the pedals so your heels rest comfortably on the floor
2. Adjust pedal spacing by moving each pedal left or right to align the mounting holes with the base
3. If using on carpet or a hard floor, support the pedals at the back to prevent them from sliding

> *"The bolt pattern also allows the bundle to be mounted on most sim rigs."* — Moza R3 Setup Guide

### 2.4 Attaching the Steering Wheel

The R3 uses Moza's quick-release system:

1. Align the 6 steel balls on the wheel's female quick-release with the 6 grooves on the base's male quick-release
2. Push the wheel toward the base until it clicks into place
3. To remove: squeeze the black quick-release part with the MOZA logo towards the wheel with both hands and pull

**Important:** The ESX wheel (Xbox version) is the only wheel that makes the R3 Xbox-compatible. The PC version (ES Lite) will not work on Xbox.

## 3. Xbox Connection

### 3.1 Step-by-Step Connection

Follow these steps in order:

```
Step 1: Connect the SR-P Lite pedal set to the PEDAL port on the R3 base
        (Use the RJ45 cable provided)

Step 2: Plug the power cord into the power adapter, then into the R3 base

Step 3: Plug the power adapter into a wall outlet

Step 4: Connect the supplied USB cable:
        - USB-B end → R3 base's USB-B port
        - USB-A end → Xbox USB port

Step 5: Turn on the R3 base by pressing the power button at the rear

Step 6: Turn on your Xbox console
```

### 3.2 Connection Indicators

| Light Pattern | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| **RPM light flows from side to center** | ✅ Successful Xbox connection |
| **RPM light is flashing** | ⚠️ You are in PC mode—switch to Xbox mode |

> *"Surpassing traditional 3 Nm bases, the R3's motor offers consistent, unadulterated force feedback that breathes life into every race."* — MOZA Racing

## 4. Mode Switching

### 4.1 Xbox vs PC Mode

The R3 can operate in two modes:

| Mode | Use For | Activation |
|------|---------|------------|
| **Xbox Mode** | Xbox Series X\|S, Xbox One | Default for Xbox connection |
| **PC Mode** | Windows PC gaming | For use with PC titles |

### 4.2 How to Switch Modes

To switch between Xbox and PC mode:

1. Press and hold the **S1** and **S2** buttons on the steering wheel simultaneously
2. Hold for **3 seconds**
3. The RPM light pattern will change to indicate the new mode

**When to Use This:**
- If the wheel is not properly detected by the Xbox
- If force feedback is not working
- When switching between Xbox and PC gaming

### 4.3 The MOZA Pit House App

The R3 supports mobile app functionality via the MOZA Racing app:

1. Download the **MOZA Racing** mobile app from your app store
2. Connect to your R3 via Bluetooth
3. Access pedal calibration, wheel settings, and firmware updates

## 5. Essential Settings

### 5.1 Steering Angle (Rotation)

The R3 allows unlimited wheel rotation. For sim racing:

| Game Type | Recommended Angle |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Forza Motorsport** | 540° - 900° |
| **Forza Horizon** | 540° - 720° |
| **F1 Series** | 360° - 540° |
| **Assetto Corsa** | 540° - 900° |

> *"Good steering angles for Forza Horizon 6 are between 540 to 900."* — Sim Racing Setup Guide

**How to Adjust:**
1. Open the MOZA Pit House app (mobile or PC)
2. Navigate to wheel settings
3. Adjust Maximum Steering Angle
4. Match this to your in-game steering angle setting

### 5.2 Force Feedback Settings (Recommended for Beginners)

| Setting | Recommended Value | What It Does |
|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Maximum Torque Limit** | 80-100% | Controls overall FFB strength |
| **Force Feedback Reversal** | Off | Prevents inverted FFB |
| **Hands-Off Protection** | On | Prevents oscillation when hands are off |
| **Maximum Steering Angle** | 720° | Rotation range |

**Start with these settings and adjust based on feel.**

### 5.3 The "Squeeze" Philosophy

> *"Smooth is fast. Abrupt is slow."* — Every race driver ever

Your R3's direct drive motor provides detailed force feedback with zero dead zone. This means:

- **Every input is communicated** — you'll feel exactly what the car is doing
- **Smooth inputs are rewarded** — jerky movements will upset the car
- **The wheel is your teacher** — learn to read what it's telling you

> *"When the car is at the limit, the steering wheel will feel heavy and precise. When it goes light, you've lost the front tires."* — Going Faster!

## 6. Pedal Calibration

### 6.1 Understanding the SR-P Lite Pedals

The SR-P Lite pedals feature:
- High-strength steel construction
- Ultra-sensitive angle sensors
- Fully adjustable pedal spacing
- Hall sensor technology (no physical contact, longer life)

### 6.2 Calibrating Your Pedals

If you encounter pedal travel issues during initial use, there are two calibration methods:

**Method 1: Calibrate in PC Mode**
1. Connect the device to a PC and enter PC mode
2. Recalibrate the pedal until it functions normally
3. Switch back to Xbox mode for regular usage

**Method 2: Calibrate with MOZA Racing Mobile App**
1. Download and install the MOZA Racing mobile app
2. Access the pedal calibration feature within the app
3. By default, the mode is set to Xbox

### 6.3 Pedal Spacing Adjustment

1. Loosen the mounting bolts on the pedal base
2. Slide each pedal left or right to your preferred position
3. Tighten the bolts securely

### 6.4 Performance Kit Upgrade (Recommended)

The SR-P Lite Performance Kit ($45 CAD) adds adjustable brake feel:

- Spring and elastomer for more brake resistance
- Much better feel compared to pressing against the Hall sensor alone
- Essential for cars without ABS where precise brake application is needed
- Install in less than five minutes

> *"A little more resistance makes the feeling much better compared to just pressing against the Hall sensor."* — Moza R3 Setup Guide

**Note:** With the Performance Kit installed, the resistance may be too great for free-standing pedals—bolting them down is recommended.

## 7. Force Feedback & The "Feel" of the Car

### 7.1 What Force Feedback Tells You

Your R3's 3.9 Nm direct drive motor communicates the car's behavior through your hands:

| Sensation | What It Means | Your Response |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| **Heavy wheel in corners** | The car has grip | You can trust the car |
| **Wheel goes light** | Front tires losing grip | Reduce steering input |
| **Wheel pulls to one side** | Uneven grip or track camber | Correct smoothly |
| **Rumble/vibration** | Road surface, curbs, bumps | Keep the wheel steady |

> *"The R3's motor offers consistent, unadulterated force feedback that breathes life into every race."* — MOZA Racing

### 7.2 Learning to Read the Wheel

> *"When the car is at the limit, the steering wheel will feel heavy and precise. When it goes light, you've lost the front tires."* — Going Faster!

**Your training goal:** Learn to read what the wheel is telling you before the car loses control.

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Progressive heaviness** | Building grip | You can turn more |
| **Sudden lightness** | Lost grip | Reduce steering, correct |
| **Consistent weight** | Stable cornering | Maintain input |
| **Vibration** | Road texture, bumps | Stay smooth |

## 9. The Apex Approach

### 9.1 The Three Fundamentals

*Going Faster!* teaches that there are three basic problems to solve in racing:

1. **Driving on the best path (The Line)**
2. **Carrying speed through corners and onto straights (Corner Exit)**
3. **Efficiently slowing the car at corner entry (Braking & Entering)**

**Your R3's direct drive feedback helps you with all three.**

### 9.2 Using FFB to Find the Line

> *"The line is the single most important concept in driving."* — Going Faster!

With your R3's detailed feedback:

1. **Feel the grip:** The wheel tells you when you're at the limit
2. **Feel the weight transfer:** Braking makes the wheel heavier as weight transfers forward
3. **Feel the slip:** The wheel goes light when tires lose grip

### 9.3 The Reference Point Method

> *"I absolutely use reference points around the track to keep on the line. It gives you consistency."* — Robbie Buhl, IndyCar Driver

**For every corner, identify:**

1. **Brake point** → Where you start braking
2. **Turn-in point** → Where you start turning
3. **Apex** → Where you're closest to the inside
4. **Track-out** → Where you exit the corner

The R3's accurate feedback helps you feel when you've hit these points correctly.

### 9.4 The Learning Progression

| Stage | Focus | When to Progress |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| **1. Hardware Familiarization** | Learn the wheel, pedals, and settings | When setup feels natural |
| **2. Track Familiarization** | Learn the layout | When you can complete 3 clean laps |
| **3. The Line** | Hit every apex | When you hit 80% of apexes |
| **4. Braking** | Consistent brake points | When you brake at the same point every lap |
| **5. Exit Speed** | Smooth throttle, max exit | When you feel comfortable |
| **6. Full Laps** | Put it all together | When you're consistent |

## 10. Troubleshooting

### 10.1 Connection Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Wheel not recognized** | Check USB connection, restart Xbox |
| **RPM light flashing** | Switch to Xbox mode (hold S1+S2 for 3 seconds) |
| **No force feedback** | Check mode (PC vs Xbox), check game settings |
| **Pedals not working** | Check RJ45 connection to base, recalibrate |

### 10.2 Force Feedback Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Wheel too heavy** | Reduce Maximum Torque Limit to 70-80% |
| **Wheel too light** | Increase Maximum Torque Limit to 100% |
| **Wheel oscillates** | Enable Hands-Off Protection |
| **No detail in FFB** | Check game's FFB settings, increase in-game FFB |

### 10.3 Common Beginner Mistakes

> *"The key word is 'little.' You'll find that the best bet is to increase your speed a little at a time, not to make a giant leap on faith alone."* — Going Faster!

| Mistake | Why It Happens | How to Fix |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| **Death grip on wheel** | Nervousness, lack of trust | Relax, hold the wheel with open hands |
| **Stabbing pedals** | Street driving habit | Practice the "squeeze" |
| **Overcorrecting** | Panic, lack of feel | Make smaller, smoother corrections |
| **Looking at the car** | Wrong instinct | Look where you want to go |
| **Spinning on exit** | Too much throttle | Squeeze more gradually |
