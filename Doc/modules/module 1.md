**Overarching Goal**  
Transition a complete beginner from reactive, chaotic driving to smooth, anticipatory, and methodical driving by mastering the three foundational pillars of racing: **The Racing Line**, **Corner Exit Speed**, and **Threshold Braking**, ultimately achieving consistent, controlled laps without relying on driving aids.

---

### **Global Baseline Setup**
**Moza R3 Wheel (Xbox) Settings:**
- **FFB Strength:** 75% (Maximizes 3.9Nm torque without clipping)
- **Damping:** 12% (Prevents wheel oscillation)
- **Natural Friction:** 5%
- **Inertia:** 5%
- **FFB Detail:** 100%
- **Road Effects:** 25% (Provides texture without overwhelming noise)

**Forza Motorsport 2023 Settings:**
- **Difficulty:** Rewind Off, ABS On (turn Off by Session 11), Traction Control Off, Stability Control Off.
- **Steering:** 900 degrees, Force Feedback 100%, Vibration Off.
- **Camera:** Hood (best for seeing nose reference points and track edges).
- **Driving Line:** Full (with braking zones) for Sessions 1-3, Racing Line only for 4-8, Off for 9-12.

### **12-Session Module (45 mins/session, ~3-4 sessions/week)**

#### **Session 1: The Foundation of "The Line"**
1. **Car/Track:** Mazda MX-5 2016 / Lime Rock Full
2. **Objectives:** Understand the relationship between radius and speed; drive smoothly without jerky inputs.
3. **Theory:** The biggest radius (R3: outside-inside-outside) yields the highest cornering speed. Smooth steering inputs preserve tire traction.
4. **Drill:** 10 mins driving at 50% speed, focusing purely on hitting the apex curb and exiting at the outside edge.
5. **Test:** Complete 3 consecutive laps within 2 seconds of each other.
6. **Challenge:** Complete a lap without the driving line visible, relying only on track edges.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Is your steering angle smooth or jagged? 2) Does your speed peak at the apex or exit? 3) Are you using 100% throttle on straights?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Are your hands feeding the wheel smoothly? 2) Are you looking through the corner to the exit? 3) Is your foot transitioning smoothly from brake to throttle?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Do you feel rushed, or are you anticipating the next turn? 2) Are you frustrated by mistakes, or analyzing them? 3) Is your breathing steady?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) What did the "outside-inside-outside" line feel like? 2) Where did you feel most uncertain? 3) What is your goal for Session 2?

#### **Session 2: Corner Exit Speed & Throttle Application**
1. **Car/Track:** Mazda MX-5 2016 / Lime Rock Full
2. **Objectives:** Learn to mix acceleration and cornering; maximize straightaway speed.
3. **Theory:** Exit speed dictates straightaway speed. Unwind the steering wheel gradually as you apply throttle to maintain traction.
4. **Drill:** Focus only on the exit of Turns 1, 3, and 5. Apply 30% throttle at the apex, increasing to 100% only as the wheel straightens.
5. **Test:** Achieve a higher top speed on the main straight than in Session 1.
6. **Challenge:** Complete a lap where the only focus is progressive throttle application; ignore ultimate lap time.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Does your throttle trace show a gradual increase or an instant spike? 2) Does your speed increase immediately after the apex? 3) Are you lifting off throttle mid-corner?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Are you unwinding the wheel as you add throttle? 2) Is the car stable, or is it understeering on exit? 3) Are you looking at the exit curb before you get there?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Are you resisting the urge to mash the throttle early? 2) Do you feel in control of the car’s weight? 3) Are you patient through the first half of the corner?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did progressive throttle change the car’s behavior? 2) Which corner exit improved the most? 3) What needs refinement?

#### **Session 3: Threshold Braking Basics**
1. **Car/Track:** Honda Civic Type R 2018 / Virginia International Raceway (VIR) Full
2. **Objectives:** Learn to brake in a straight line at 100% capacity using reference points.
3. **Theory:** Threshold braking is braking at the edge of wheel lockup/ABS intervention. Brake 100% in a straight line before turning the wheel.
4. **Drill:** Pick a fixed reference point (e.g., a tree or sign) before Oak Tree and Oak Bend. Brake hard in a straight line, release, then turn.
5. **Test:** Consistently hit the same braking marker for 5 consecutive laps.
6. **Challenge:** Brake as late as possible without triggering ABS (or minimizing ABS pulsing).
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Is your brake trace a flat, hard line, or a gradual ramp? 2) Are you braking while the steering angle is >0? 3) How much speed do you shed before turn-in?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Are you stomping or progressively squeezing the brake pedal? 2) Are your eyes on the reference point, not the speedometer? 3) Is the car stable under heavy braking?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Does late braking cause panic, or calm focus? 2) Are you trusting your reference points? 3) Are you prepared for the turn before you release the brake?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did threshold braking feel compared to casual braking? 2) Which reference points worked best? 3) What is your biggest braking fear?

#### **Session 4: Introduction to Trail Braking (Brake & Turn)**
1. **Car/Track:** Honda Civic Type R 2018 / VIR Full
2. **Objectives:** Understand mixing braking and cornering forces to carry higher entry speed.
3. **Theory:** As you turn the wheel, gradually reduce brake pressure. This keeps weight on the front tires, increasing turn-in grip and rotating the car.
4. **Drill:** Enter Turn 1 (Oak Tree) braking in a straight line, then trail off the brake smoothly as you turn to the apex.
5. **Test:** Complete 3 laps where brake pressure visibly tapers off in the telemetry during turn-in.
6. **Challenge:** Gain 0.5 seconds on Sector 1 compared to Session 3 using trail braking.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Does your brake trace taper off as steering angle increases? 2) Is your minimum speed higher at the apex than in Session 3? 3) Are you off the brake completely by the apex?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Is your left foot (brake) smoothly releasing? 2) Does the car rotate nicely, or does it understeer? 3) Are you turning in at the correct point?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Are you confident carrying more speed into the corner? 2) Do you feel the car’s weight shift to the front? 3) Are you avoiding the "stab and turn" panic reflex?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did trail braking change the car’s rotation? 2) Was it difficult to coordinate brake release and steering input? 3) What will you focus on next time?

#### **Session 5: The Apex and Geometric Line**
1. **Car/Track:** Subaru BRZ 2013 / Laguna Seca
2. **Objectives:** Differentiate between early, geometric, and late apexes based on the following straight.
3. **Theory:** The "best" line isn't always the biggest radius. A late apex allows earlier, straighter throttle application onto a long straight (e.g., exiting the Corkscrew).
4. **Drill:** Run the Corkscrew (Turns 8-11) focusing on a late apex to maximize exit speed onto the downhill straight.
5. **Test:** Hit the outside edge of the track perfectly on the exit of the Corkscrew for 3 consecutive laps.
6. **Challenge:** Drive the entire lap using only late apexes where a straight follows, and geometric apexes for isolated corners.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Where does your steering angle peak relative to the apex? 2) Is your throttle application earlier on late-apex corners? 3) Does your speed trace show a smoother, earlier climb on exit?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Are you delaying turn-in for late apexes? 2) Are you patient, letting the car drift to the inside? 3) Is your steering input sharper but shorter in duration?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Does a late apex feel "too tight" initially? 2) Are you looking far ahead to the exit, not just the apex? 3) Are you sacrificing corner entry speed for exit speed willingly?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did the late apex affect your exit speed? 2) Which corners at Laguna Seca demand a late apex? 3) What felt unnatural about this technique?

#### **Session 6: Downshifting and Weight Transfer**
1. **Car/Track:** Subaru BRZ 2013 / Laguna Seca
2. **Objectives:** Master smooth downshifts with throttle blips to avoid upsetting the car’s balance.
3. **Theory:** Downshifting without blipping locks the rear wheels, causing instability. A throttle "blip" matches engine RPM to wheel speed, keeping the car balanced.
4. **Drill:** Approach Turn 2. Brake, blip the throttle, downshift to 2nd, and turn in smoothly. Repeat for 10 laps.
5. **Test:** Complete 5 laps with zero rear-wheel chirp or ABS activation from downshifts.
6. **Challenge:** Execute a perfect downshift blip while trail braking into the Corkscrew.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Does your RPM spike briefly during the downshift? 2) Is there a sudden drop in speed or yaw spike during the shift? 3) Are you completing the shift before maximum steering angle?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Is your right foot splitting between brake and throttle smoothly? 2) Is the clutch release (if using one) or shift execution seamless? 3) Is the car stable, or does the rear step out?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Are you rushing the downshift? 2) Do you feel the mechanical sympathy for the car? 3) Are you anticipating the gear needed before the corner?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did blipping change the car’s stability? 2) Was the footwork difficult to coordinate? 3) What is your downshift success rate?

#### **Session 7: Compound Corners & Transitions**
1. **Car/Track:** Ford Mustang GT 2018 / Road America
2. **Objectives:** Learn to sacrifice one corner’s ideal line to set up the next, more important corner.
3. **Theory:** In a chicane or compound corner (e.g., The Kink into Carousel), the exit of the final corner matters most. Adjust the first corner’s exit to straighten the second.
4. **Drill:** Focus on Turns 5, 6, and 7. Take a tight, early apex in Turn 5 to be perfectly positioned for a wide, fast exit out of Turn 7.
5. **Test:** Maintain a higher minimum speed through the compound section than in previous attempts.
6. **Challenge:** Complete a lap prioritizing only the exit speed of corners that lead onto a straight, ignoring standalone corners.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Is your line through the compound section asymmetrical? 2) Do you avoid mid-corner adjustments between the linked turns? 3) Is your throttle application earlier on the final exit?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Are you using minimal steering input between the linked corners? 2) Are you looking two corners ahead? 3) Is your car balanced, or are you fighting understeer?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Does it feel wrong to "ruin" the first corner’s line? 2) Are you thinking ahead, or just reacting to the immediate turn? 3) Do you feel the rhythm of the compound section?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did sacrificing Turn 5 help Turn 7? 2) Where did you struggle with car positioning? 3) How will you apply this to other tracks?

#### **Session 8: Managing Understeer & Oversteer**
1. **Car/Track:** Ford Mustang GT 2018 / Road America
2. **Objectives:** Recognize and correct loss of traction without panicking.
3. **Theory:** Understeer (front loses grip): reduce steering angle, ease off throttle. Oversteer (rear loses grip): steer into the slide, smooth throttle. Abruptly lifting off throttle causes snap oversteer.
4. **Drill:** Deliberately induce mild understeer on exit of Turn 3 by applying throttle too early, then correct by straightening the wheel slightly.
5. **Test:** Complete 3 laps with zero off-track excursions, actively managing minor slides.
6. **Challenge:** Drive 5 laps with Traction Control completely off, managing throttle carefully to prevent wheelspin.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Do your steering and throttle inputs spike during corrections? 2) Does your speed drop significantly when correcting understeer? 3) Is your yaw rate stable, or oscillating?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Are your corrections smooth and subtle? 2) Are you avoiding the "lift-off" panic reflex? 3) Are your hands uncrossed and ready to catch a slide?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Do you panic when the car slides, or do you assess? 2) Are you respecting the car’s limits, or fighting them? 3) Is your grip on the wheel relaxed or white-knuckled?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) What did understeer feel like in the Mustang? 2) How did you correct it? 3) What is your confidence level in car control?

#### **Session 9: Advanced Trail Braking & Rotation**
1. **Car/Track:** Porsche 718 Cayman GTS 2018 / Maple Valley
2. **Objectives:** Use trail braking actively to rotate the car’s rear end for tighter corners.
3. **Theory:** Keeping light brake pressure deep into the turn shifts weight forward, lightening the rear and helping the car point toward the apex (rotation).
4. **Drill:** Turn 3 (the tight right-hander). Brake late, trail brake deeply, and feel the car pivot around the front tires.
5. **Test:** Achieve a cleaner, tighter apex without running wide on exit, using trail braking to rotate.
6. **Challenge:** Complete a lap where every slow corner utilizes trail braking for rotation, verified by smooth telemetry.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Does your brake trace extend past the initial steering input? 2) Is your steering angle lower at the apex due to better rotation? 3) Is your throttle application earlier and smoother?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Is your brake release feather-light and progressive? 2) Are you feeling the rear of the car step in slightly? 3) Are you avoiding abrupt brake release?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Are you confident in the car’s front-end grip? 2) Do you feel like you are "placing" the car, not just driving it? 3) Are you anticipating the rotation, not surprised by it?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did trail braking improve your apex speed? 2) Was it easy to find the limit of rear rotation? 3) What adjustments are needed?

#### **Session 10: Consistency and Mental Anticipation**
1. **Car/Track:** Porsche 718 Cayman GTS 2018 / Maple Valley
2. **Objectives:** Build a mental map of the track; prioritize anticipation over reaction.
3. **Theory:** Fast drivers don't have faster reactions; they anticipate. They know exactly what the car will do and have a plan for every inch of the track.
4. **Drill:** Drive 5 laps with your eyes focusing 2-3 seconds ahead of the car, calling out the next reference point aloud.
5. **Test:** Complete 5 consecutive laps within a 1.5-second window.
6. **Challenge:** Drive a full lap with the in-game driving line turned completely OFF, relying solely on memory and reference points.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) How tightly clustered are your lap time and sector time traces? 2) Are your braking points identical across laps? 3) Is your steering trace consistent lap after lap?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Are your inputs automatic and smooth? 2) Are you looking ahead, or staring at the hood? 3) Are you executing the plan, or reacting to mistakes?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Does the track feel like it’s in "slow motion"? 2) Are you calm and focused, or tense? 3) Do you feel a sense of rhythm and flow?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did anticipation change your driving feel? 2) Where did you break consistency, and why? 3) Are you ready to remove driving aids?

#### **Session 11: Removing the Training Wheels**
1. **Car/Track:** Mazda MX-5 2016 / Suzuka Full
2. **Objectives:** Drive without ABS and Traction Control, feeling the raw limits of the tires.
3. **Theory:** Aids mask mistakes. Driving without them requires precise threshold braking and progressive throttle to manage the traction circle manually.
4. **Drill:** Focus on Turn 1 (the sweeping right). Brake without ABS, feeling for the threshold, and apply throttle progressively on exit without wheelspin.
5. **Test:** Complete 3 clean laps with ABS and TC Off, with no major spins or lockups.
6. **Challenge:** Match or beat your Session 10 lap time, but with all assists off.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Are there any sudden spikes in brake pressure indicating lockup? 2) Does your throttle trace show wheelspin (RPM spike without speed increase)? 3) Is your steering smooth, or are you correcting slides?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Are you modulating the brake pedal, not stomping? 2) Are you gentle on the throttle application? 3) Are you catching slides with smooth counter-steer?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Does the lack of aids make you anxious or liberated? 2) Are you respecting the car’s limits more? 3) Are you focused on smoothness over brute force?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) How did the car feel without ABS/TC? 2) What bad habits did the aids previously hide? 3) How has your car control improved?

#### **Session 12: Final Assessment & The "Fast" Lap**
1. **Car/Track:** Subaru BRZ 2013 / Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps
2. **Objectives:** Synthesize all skills (line, exit speed, trail braking, anticipation) into one cohesive, fast, and safe lap.
3. **Theory:** The framework of fast laps is built on a foundation of smoothness, the correct line, maximizing exit speed, and threshold/trail braking. Bravery is not speed; skill is speed.
4. **Drill:** 15 mins of free practice, focusing on your weakest sector identified in previous sessions.
5. **Test:** Set a "qualifying" lap. This lap must be clean, smooth, and represent your absolute best execution of all 11 prior sessions.
6. **Challenge:** Record the lap and compare it to a professional telemetry trace (or Forza ghost) to identify one final area for future growth.
7. **Telemetry Qs:** 1) Does your lap show a smooth, continuous flow of braking, turning, and accelerating? 2) Are your minimum corner speeds optimized? 3) Is your exit speed maximized onto the Kemmel Straight?
8. **Technique Qs:** 1) Was your steering smooth and deliberate? 2) Did you use trail braking effectively at Eau Rouge/Radillion? 3) Was your throttle application progressive and controlled?
9. **Mental Qs:** 1) Did you feel calm and in control during your fast lap? 2) Were you anticipating the track, or reacting to it? 3) Do you feel proud of your progress from Day 1?
10. **Reflection Qs:** 1) What is the biggest lesson you learned in this 30-day module? 2) How has your understanding of "fast" changed? 3) What is your goal for Module 2?
