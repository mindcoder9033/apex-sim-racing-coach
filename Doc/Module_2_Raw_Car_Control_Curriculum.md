## Executive Summary & Assist Removal Progression Strategy

Module 2 transforms a beginner driver from relying on electronic safety nets to achieving **100% raw car control**. Electronic assists are peeled back systematically across the 4 weeks in lockstep with Skip Barber theoretical concepts:

```
WEEK 1: Racing Line & Reference Points ──────► Driving Line OFF  (ABS ON, TCS/STM ON)
WEEK 2: Exit Speed & Chassis Balance ────────► TCS & STM OFF     (Driving Line OFF, ABS ON)
WEEK 3: Braking, Lockups & Weight Transfer ──► ABS OFF          (Driving Line OFF, TCS/STM OFF)
WEEK 4: 100% Raw Control Masterclass ────────► ALL ASSISTS OFF   (Line OFF, TCS/STM OFF, ABS OFF)
```

## Standard 45-Minute Session Formula

Every session follows a strict 8-step structure:

1. **Theory (5 mins)**: Concept briefing & Skip Barber physics breakdown.
2. **Practice (5 mins)**: Track warm-up & visual/pedal feel calibration.
3. **Practical (10 mins)**: Focused cornering drill execution.
4. **Challenge (5 mins)**: Gamified constraint / target test.
5. **Assessment (5 mins)**: Timed/consistency benchmark run.
6. **Telemetry & Replay Study (6 mins)**: Visual replay camera inspection (Apex precision, body roll, line).
7. **Psychological Reflection (4 mins)**: Focus check, frustration audit, reset prompt.
8. **Session Reflection (5 mins)**: Scoring, key takeaways, next session prep.

# Week 1: The Racing Line & Reference Points (Driving Line OFF)

### Session 1: Line Fundamentals & Arc Maximization
- **Focus**: Corner radius physics ($15GR = m.p.h.^2$) & turning OFF Driving Line
- **Assists Config**: Driving Line OFF | TCS ON | STM ON | ABS ON | Steering Normal
- **Theory**:
  - Maximizing corner radius increases maximum achievable cornering speed.
  - Driving the outside-inside-outside racing line increases corner arc radius by **89%**, delivering a **37% speed advantage** over hugging the inside curb.
- **Practice Drills**:
  - *Easy*: Turn 1 Arc Feel (70 km/h inside vs outside line comparison, 5m)
  - *Medium*: Visual Reference Spotting (Identifying turn-in, apex, track-out points, 8m)
  - *Hard*: Line Arc Sweep without line guide (4 clean laps, 10m)
- **Practical**: 5 laps at Lime Rock Park using full track width.
- **Challenge**: Apex Curb Precision — Clip within 1 foot of apex curbs on T1, T3, and T7 5 times in a row.
- **Assessment**: Demonstrated corner radius maximization without driving line guide (Passing Score: 80).
- **Telemetry & Replay Study**: Inspect camera views to verify outside tires are within 1 foot of track edge at turn-in and track-out.
- **Psychological Reflection**: Driving without green arrows requires trusting your eyes looking far ahead down the track.
- **Session Reflection**: Record lap times and track width usage notes.

### Session 2: The Apex Spectrum — Early vs. Late Apex
- **Focus**: Understanding line errors & late apex safety
- **Assists Config**: Driving Line OFF | TCS ON | STM ON | ABS ON | Steering Normal
- **Theory**:
  - Turning in too early forces a tight radius at corner exit, pinching your trajectory and forcing you off track or off throttle.
  - Symptom of early apexing: Having to turn the wheel harder AFTER passing the apex.
  - A late apex opens up the exit radius and allows earlier full throttle.
- **Practice Drills**:
  - *Easy*: Early Apex Pinch Feel (Intentionally turning in early at Turn 3, 5m)
  - *Medium*: Late Apex Exit Squeeze (Turning in 3m later at Turn 3, 8m)
  - *Hard*: Late Apex Precision Sweep (All 7 corners, 10m)
- **Practical**: 5 laps focusing on late turn-in points at Turn 3 and Turn 7.
- **Challenge**: Complete 4 consecutive late-apex laps with zero post-apex steering increases.
- **Assessment**: Late apex placement demonstrated (Passing Score: 80).
- **Telemetry & Replay Study**: Verify inside tire touches apex curb at midpoint/late point of corner.
- **Psychological Reflection**: Waiting that extra fraction of a second before turn-in requires patience. Late turn-in equals fast exit!
- **Session Reflection**: Record exit speed comparisons.

### Session 3: Visual Reference Point Mastery

# Week 2: Corner Exit Speed & Chassis Balance (TCS & STM OFF)

### Session 4: Corner Exit Speed & Disabling TCS / STM
- **Focus**: Longitudinal weight transfer & turning OFF Traction Control & Stability Control
- **Assists Config**: Driving Line OFF | TCS OFF | STM OFF | ABS ON | Steering Simulation
- **Theory**:
  - Weight shifts rearward under acceleration, increasing rear tire traction.
  - Abrupt throttle stabs unload front tires (understeer) or break rear traction (spin).
  - TCS OFF Rule: Squeeze throttle over 2 seconds as steering wheel unwinds.
- **Practice Drills**:
  - *Easy*: Standing Start Throttle Squeeze (5m)
  - *Medium*: Steering Unwind & Throttle Sync (8m)
  - *Hard*: TCS-Free Clean Lap Sweep (10m)
- **Practical**: 5 laps managing rear traction with foot control.
- **Challenge**: Complete 4 consecutive clean laps without rear wheelspin or spins.
- **Assessment**: TCS/STM removal control demonstrated (Passing Score: 80).
- **Telemetry & Replay Study**: Audit rear fishtailing and throttle squeeze linearity.
- **Psychological Reflection**: Your foot is the traction control computer. Stay calm on throttle application.
- **Session Reflection**: Log pedal modulation notes.

### Session 5: Oversteer & Trailing Throttle Balance
- **Focus**: Lift-off oversteer mechanics & Correction-Pause-Recovery
- **Assists Config**: Driving Line OFF | TCS OFF | STM OFF | ABS ON | Steering Simulation
- **Theory**:
  - Trailing Throttle Oversteer (TTO): Sudden throttle lift mid-corner unloads rear tires, causing rear end slide.
  - Skip Barber Recovery: 1) Correction (opposite lock), 2) Pause, 3) Recovery (unwind).
- **Practice Drills**:
  - *Easy*: Intentional Throttle Lift Rotation (5m)
  - *Medium*: Correction-Pause-Recovery Feel (8m)
  - *Hard*: Oversteer Control Stint (10m)
- **Practical**: 6 laps practicing throttle balance and opposite lock corrections.
- **Challenge**: Catch and recover 3 rear slides without spinning or leaving the track.
- **Assessment**: Oversteer recovery execution (Passing Score: 80).
- **Telemetry & Replay Study**: Check opposite lock angle and unwind timing in replay.
- **Psychological Reflection**: Oversteer is feedback from the chassis, not a failure.
- **Session Reflection**: Record slide recovery confidence score.

### Session 6: Maximizing Straightaway Speed (Exit Priority)
- **Focus**: Exit speed compounding & Slow-In / Fast-Out philosophy
- **Assists Config**: Driving Line OFF | TCS OFF | STM OFF | ABS ON | Steering Simulation
- **Theory**:
  - Exit speed compounds over the entire length of the straightaway.
  - Slow-In, Fast-Out: Sacrificing 1 m.p.h. on entry lets you pick up 100% throttle at the apex for maximum straightaway launch.
- **Practice Drills**:
  - *Easy*: Slow-In / Fast-Out Entry Line (5m)

- **Assessment**: Exit speed compounding demonstrated (Passing Score: 80).
- **Telemetry & Replay Study**: Verify 100% throttle location in replay view.
- **Psychological Reflection**: Patience on entry pays massive dividends down the straight.
- **Session Reflection**: Record top speeds and lap times.

# Week 3: Braking, Lockups & Weight Transfer (ABS OFF)

- **Focus**: Threshold braking mechanics & turning OFF ABS
- **Assists Config**: Driving Line OFF | TCS OFF | STM OFF | ABS OFF | Steering Simulation

### Session 8: Brake Lockup Recovery & Modulation
- **Focus**: Feathering pedal to re-start tire rotation & steering authority
- **Assists Config**: Driving Line OFF | TCS OFF | STM OFF | ABS OFF | Steering Simulation
- **Theory**:
  - Pressing harder during a lockup makes the slide worse.
  - Relief Modulation: Ease off pedal by 15-20% to re-start tire rotation, then re-apply at 80% threshold.
- **Practice Drills**:
  - *Easy*: Intentional Lockup & Release (5m)
  - *Medium*: Feather Modulation Drill (8m)
  - *Hard*: Lockup-Free Turn 1 & 5 Sweep (10m)
- **Practical**: 6 laps practicing rapid lockup detection and relief modulation.

- **Assessment**: Immediate pedal pressure modulation demonstrated (Passing Score: 80).
- **Telemetry & Replay Study**: Audit lockup duration and turn-in control in replay.
- **Psychological Reflection**: Relieving brake pressure takes discipline. Stay sensitive in your foot.
- **Session Reflection**: Document modulation reaction times.

### Session 9: Trail-Braking & Weight Transfer Entry
- **Focus**: Blending brake release with turn-in to load front tires
- **Assists Config**: Driving Line OFF | TCS OFF | STM OFF | ABS OFF | Steering Simulation
- **Theory**:
  - Trail-braking carries light, diminishing brake pressure past turn-in to the apex.
  - Keeps 250+ lbs of weight on front tires, maximizing turn-in grip and rotation.
  - As steering angle increases, brake pressure must diminish linearly.
- **Practice Drills**:
  - *Easy*: Straight-Line vs Trail-Brake Comparison (5m)
  - *Medium*: Linear Pressure Bleed to Apex (8m)
  - *Hard*: Trail-Braking Sweep T1 & T5 (10m)

- **Challenge**: Execute smooth trail-braking with clean rotation across 5 consecutive laps.
- **Assessment**: Linear brake pressure bleed synchronized with turn-in (Passing Score: 85).
- **Telemetry & Replay Study**: Verify brake release reaches zero exactly at apex curb.
- **Psychological Reflection**: Trail-braking is an art of finesse. Feel front tire weight in the wheel.
- **Session Reflection**: Record trail-braking depth notes.
